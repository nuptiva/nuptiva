Curious to know more about who I am and how I work? Here's a little insight into my world and my approach.

Fiona Colon

WEDDING PLANNER & WEDDING DESIGNER

My name is Fiona, and I am the passionate founder of Nuptiva Wedding. A true lover of love and the joyous moments it brings, I have dedicated my career to creating weddings that embody the elegance of French style and the magic of life's most precious moments.

For me, every wedding is a new story to tell, a masterpiece to create. I have a deep respect for detail and an endless admiration for moments of emotion and joy. My caring and personalized approach allows me to work closely with you to ensure that every element of your special day perfectly reflects your vision and personality.

With a boundless creativity, my goal is to turn your dreams into a sublime celebration marked by refinement, authenticity, and the timeless elegance of France.
